How do I quickly clean a patio or balcony floor?

Short answer: Sweep first, then spray the floor from the far end toward the drain. Rinsing in one direction stops dirt from coming back.

What to do

  1. Sweep away loose debris before using water.
  2. Spray from the far side toward the drain.
  3. Use a floor cleaner for greasy marks.
  4. Let the floor dry before use and watch for slippery spots.

Questions people ask

Should I sweep or spray first?

Sweep first. Water turns dust into mud.

Is using lots of water a problem in an apartment?

Make sure the balcony drains properly and will not drip onto neighbors below. Use controlled amounts.

Does a spray gun remove old stains?

Not by itself. Old stains need scrubbing and a suitable cleaner.

How do I avoid slippery floors?

Dry the area and wear shoes with a firm sole.

Is it safe for tile and stone?

Water rinsing is generally safe, but cleaners differ by surface, so check before using a new product.
