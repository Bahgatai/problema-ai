How can I wash baby clothes separately from the family laundry?

Short answer: Washing small batches separately keeps baby clothes apart from the rest. A small washer lets you do that without running a full machine. Check the detergent and rinse well.

What to do

  1. Use a gentle detergent made for babies.
  2. Rinse more than once so no detergent stays in the fabric.
  3. Check the care label on each garment.
  4. Dry in a clean place.
