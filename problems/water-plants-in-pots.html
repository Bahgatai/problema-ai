How do I water pots and balcony plants evenly?

Short answer: Use a gentle spray, water slowly until it drains from the bottom, and avoid soaking the leaves in strong sun.

What to do

  1. Use a soft spray pattern.
  2. Water until it drains from the pot's base.
  3. Empty saucers so roots do not sit in water.
  4. Water in the morning or evening.
