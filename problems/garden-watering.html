How can I water my garden without wasting water?

Short answer: A spray with controllable flow lets you water the soil where it is needed instead of flooding one spot. A trigger lets you stop the water between plants.

What to do

  1. Water early in the morning or late afternoon to reduce evaporation.
  2. Aim at the soil near the roots rather than the leaves when you can.
  3. Use a gentle spray for seedlings and a stronger one for paths and beds.
  4. Shut the flow off while you move between plants.

Questions people ask

What is the best time to water plants?

Early morning or late afternoon, because strong sun evaporates much of the water.

Can a strong spray harm seedlings?

Yes. Use a soft, wider spray for seedlings and keep the stronger setting for hard surfaces.

Does a spray gun save water?

It can help because you can stop the flow easily. The actual saving depends on how you use it and we have not measured it.

Can I also use it to rinse patios and balconies?

Yes, spraying helps with light dust. Old stains need scrubbing and cleaner.

Does it fit any hose?

Not always. Connectors differ, so check the listing. You may need an adapter.
