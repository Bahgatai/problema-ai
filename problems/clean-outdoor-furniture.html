How do I clean dusty outdoor furniture?

Short answer: Wipe off loose dust, spray with water, scrub with mild soap, and rinse. Let it dry before you use it.

What to do

  1. Brush off loose dust and leaves.
  2. Wet the surface with a moderate spray.
  3. Scrub with mild soap and a soft brush.
  4. Rinse and let dry in the sun or shade.

Check the care label first. Many cushions should not be soaked.

Does a spray gun get rid of stains?

It rinses. Stains need scrubbing and the right cleaner.

Wood or plastic furniture?

Plastic tolerates water well. For wood, use light water and dry quickly.
