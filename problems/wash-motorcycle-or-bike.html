How do I wash a motorcycle or bicycle at home?

Short answer: Rinse loose dirt with a gentle spray, wash with soap and a soft brush, and rinse again. Keep strong spray away from bearings, chains and electrical parts.

What to do

  1. Rinse mud and dust with a gentle spray first.
  2. Wash with mild soap and a soft brush.
  3. Avoid aiming strong water at bearings, electronics and open connectors.
  4. Dry and lubricate the chain afterwards.

Questions people ask

Can I use a hose spray gun on a motorcycle?

Yes for rinsing, using a gentle pattern. Keep spray away from sensitive parts.

Will water damage the bike?

It can if forced into bearings or electrics. Use light spray and dry the bike afterwards.

Does a spray gun clean mud?

It helps loosen it. Stubborn mud needs a brush.

Do I need a pressure washer?

No for basic cleaning. Not having one is often safer for bikes.
