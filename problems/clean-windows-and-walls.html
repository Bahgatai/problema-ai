How do I rinse dusty windows and exterior walls?

Short answer: Rinse dust from the top down with a moderate spray, then wipe the glass. Avoid strong jets on window seals and old paint.

What to do

  1. Rinse from top to bottom.
  2. Use a soft spray on windows and seals.
  3. Wipe glass with a squeegee or microfiber cloth.
  4. Skip walls with peeling paint or cracks.
