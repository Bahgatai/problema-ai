How do I stop wipers from freezing to the glass?

Short answer: Lift the wipers off the glass overnight or cover them. A cover that includes the wiper area can help, if the listing says so.

What to do

  1. Lift wipers away from the glass overnight.
  2. Cover the wiper area if you can.
  3. Do not force a frozen wiper.
  4. Use de-icer.
