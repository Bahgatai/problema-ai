How can I do laundry while traveling or on a long stay?

Short answer: For long trips or temporary housing, a portable washer reduces trips to laundromats. Make sure the place has power and a drain and that you are fine washing small loads.

What to do

  1. Wash light clothing rather than bulky items.
  2. Bring liquid detergent suited to small machines.
  3. Check the local voltage. You may need a plug adapter or converter.
  4. Hang clothes to dry if the spin bucket is not enough.
