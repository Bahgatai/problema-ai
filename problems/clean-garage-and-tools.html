How do I wash a garage floor and garden tools?

Short answer: Sweep, spray off dirt, scrub oil with a degreaser, and rinse toward a drain. Dry metal tools to prevent rust.

What to do

  1. Sweep up debris first.
  2. Spray surfaces to loosen dirt.
  3. Use a degreaser on oil marks.
  4. Dry tools and wipe with a light oil if needed.
