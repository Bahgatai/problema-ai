How do I keep my car cooler when parked in the sun?

Short answer: Covering the windshield reduces direct sun on the dashboard and seats, so the cabin heats up less. How much it helps depends on the cover and the weather.

What to do

  1. Park in the shade when possible.
  2. Put the cover on the windshield before leaving the car.
  3. Crack the windows slightly if it is safe.
  4. Take heat-sensitive items out of the car.
