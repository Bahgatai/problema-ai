How do I protect my dashboard from sun damage?

Short answer: Reducing direct sun lowers heat and fading on the dashboard. A windshield cover is one way. Parking in shade also helps.

What to do

  1. Park in shade if possible.
  2. Cover the windshield when parked.
  3. Use a dashboard protector if you like.
  4. Keep it clean.
