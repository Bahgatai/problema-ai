Does a cover help against small falling debris?

Short answer: A thin cover is not hail protection. It may keep small debris off the glass, but it will not stop a hard impact.

What to do

  1. Use covered parking for hail.
  2. Remove debris carefully.
  3. Do not rely on a thin cover for impacts.
  4. Check insurance coverage.
