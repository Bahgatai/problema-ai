How do I rinse a dog or pet outdoors?

Short answer: Use a gentle spray at a comfortable water temperature, avoid the face, and use a pet shampoo. Keep the spray soft because strong jets can scare or hurt animals.

What to do

  1. Use lukewarm water and a gentle spray setting.
  2. Keep water away from eyes, ears and nose.
  3. Use a shampoo made for pets.
  4. Rinse until no soap remains, then dry the animal.

Questions people ask

Is a spray gun safe for pets?

Only on a soft setting. A strong jet can scare or hurt an animal.

What water temperature should I use?

Lukewarm. Check it with your hand first.

Do I need special shampoo?

Use one made for pets. Human shampoo can irritate some animals.
