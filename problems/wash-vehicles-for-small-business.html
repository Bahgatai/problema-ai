Can a spray gun help with washing delivery bikes or work vehicles?

Short answer: For light, regular cleaning, a hose and a spray gun are a low-cost option. For heavy grease or large fleets, a commercial setup is more suitable.

What to do

  1. Rinse dirt before it dries.
  2. Use proper vehicle shampoo.
  3. Avoid harsh chemicals on paint.
  4. Plan the water supply and drainage.
