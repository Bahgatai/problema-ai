What can I use for laundry if I have no space for a washing machine?

Short answer: In a dorm, a single room or a small apartment, a small foldable washer can be stored away after use. The trade-off is small capacity, so you wash small batches.

What to do

  1. Measure the space you have before ordering.
  2. Estimate your weekly load and compare it with the stated capacity (6L or 11L depending on the listing).
  3. Check where you will get power and drainage.
  4. Add shipping and possible import duties to the cost.
