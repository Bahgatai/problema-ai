How do I protect my windshield from dust, leaves and bird droppings?

Short answer: A cover over the glass keeps dust, leaves and droppings off it, and you shake or wipe the cover instead of scrubbing the glass. The title says waterproof, but how well it works is not confirmed by us.

What to do

  1. Put the cover on before leaving the car for a long time.
  2. Shake it away from the car so dirt does not land on the paint.
  3. Clean the cover from time to time.
  4. Avoid parking under trees where birds gather if you can.
