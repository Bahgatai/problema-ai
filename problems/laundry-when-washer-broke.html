What can I do when my washing machine breaks down?

Short answer: Wash small essentials by hand or with a small washer while you wait for repair. Heavy items like blankets go to a laundromat.

What to do

  1. Prioritize underwear, socks and work clothes.
  2. Use a small washer or hand wash.
  3. Take bulky items to a laundromat.
  4. Get a repair quote for the main machine.
