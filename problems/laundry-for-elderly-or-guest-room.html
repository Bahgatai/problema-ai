Is a small washer useful for a guest room or elderly relative?

Short answer: Small, light loads can be easier than a large machine. Check weight, controls and whether the person can handle water filling and emptying.

What to do

  1. Ask whether they can handle filling and emptying.
  2. Place it near water and power.
  3. Keep detergents labeled.
