How do I wash my car at home without going to a car wash?

Short answer: You can wash a car at home with a hose and a spray gun that controls the flow. Work from the top down and rinse often. It saves time at the car wash, but a hose-end gun will not give you pressure-washer power.

What to do

  1. Rinse the car first so loose sand and dust come off before you rub.
  2. Use car shampoo and a clean sponge or mitt. Avoid strong household cleaners on paint.
  3. Wash in the shade if you can so water does not dry into spots.
  4. Rinse foam from top to bottom, then dry with a microfiber towel.

Questions people ask

Do I need a pressure washer to wash my car at home?

No. A hose and a spray gun are enough for light washing and rinsing. Caked mud may need more rubbing.

What does a spray gun add to a hose?

Easier control of the spray pattern and an easy on/off while you work. It does not usually raise your supply pressure.

Can washing with a hose damage paint?

Normal washing with water and proper car shampoo is generally safe. Damage mostly comes from rubbing dry dust or using harsh cleaners.

How often should I wash my car?

There is no fixed number. Every two to four weeks is common, sooner after bird droppings or mud.

Should I use hot water?

Cool or room-temperature water is the safer choice. Hot water can leave spots on glass and paint.
