How do I stop frost or snow from building up on my windshield overnight?

Short answer: In cold climates, a cover placed on the windshield overnight keeps frost and snow off the glass, and you remove the cover instead of scraping. Make sure it fits your windshield and is held down.

What to do

  1. Fit the cover in the evening and secure it so wind cannot lift it.
  2. Remove snow from the cover before pulling it off.
  3. Never pour boiling water on cold glass.
  4. Dry the cover before storing it.
