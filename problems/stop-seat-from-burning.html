Why do car seats get so hot and what helps?

Short answer: Direct sun heats the cabin. Reducing sun through the windshield helps, as does parking in shade and airing out the car.

What to do

  1. Use a windshield cover.
  2. Park in shade.
  3. Open doors for a minute to let heat out.
  4. Use seat covers if you like.
